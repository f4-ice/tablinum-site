Common questions

Where is my data, and how do I back it up?

In the data folder you chose when you first opened Tablinum. The folder icon at the top right of the app shows where it is and opens it in Finder. To back it up, copy that folder, or keep it somewhere that is backed up already.

Tablinum asks me to choose my folder again. Is my data gone?

No. macOS only lets Tablinum use a folder you have picked, and it sometimes forgets that permission, for example after the folder was moved to another disk. Click “Open existing folder…” and pick the same folder. Nothing in it is changed.

How do I move my data to another folder or another Mac?

Quit Tablinum, move or copy the whole data folder, then open Tablinum, go to Settings (the gear icon), click “Open existing folder…” and pick the folder in its new place.

A link to a file says Tablinum cannot reach it.

macOS only lets Tablinum open files and folders you have picked yourself. Open the card, right-click the link, choose “Edit link…” and pick the file again with “Choose a file…” or “Choose a folder…”. This is also needed once after moving your data to another Mac.

I typed a path such as Documents/report.pdf and it is not a link.

That is by design. Web addresses become links as you type them, but a file or folder has to be picked: select the words, click the link icon above the text, and choose the file or folder.

My AI assistant says Tablinum is not open.

The assistant works through the Tablinum app, so the app has to be running. Open Tablinum and ask again. If the assistant cannot find Tablinum at all, follow “Connect an AI assistant” in the app once more; the steps there always match your copy of the app.

I deleted something by mistake.

Press ⌘Z to undo your last change; you can go back up to fifty steps. Deleted cards, lists and boards also wait in the trash (the bin icon), where they can be restored until the trash is emptied.

A card keeps jumping back when I drag it.

The list has a script with a SORT command, which keeps its cards in order. A small sort icon next to the list's title shows this. Open the list's menu and choose “Edit script…” to change or remove it.

Which Macs does Tablinum run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 12 or later.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. If something in the app is not working for you, please write first; it can often be fixed.
