Privacy policy

Your work stays on your Mac.

Tablinum does not collect, send or sell anything about you. This page explains what that means in practice.

Last updated 11 October 2026

The short version

  • Tablinum collects no personal data and has no accounts.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • The app makes no connection to the internet.
  • Everything you make is saved as files in a folder you choose on your Mac.

What Tablinum stores, and where

Your boards, lists, cards and notes are saved as plain Markdown files, with their attachments next to them, in the data folder you choose when you first open the app. You can move, copy, back up or delete that folder whenever you like.

A few settings are kept in the app's own private folder on your Mac: which data folder to use, your default font, how long the trash is kept, and the permissions that let the app open the files and folders you have linked from a card.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. The developer has no way to see it.

What Tablinum can reach on your Mac

Tablinum runs inside the macOS App Sandbox. It can only read and write the data folder you picked, and the individual files and folders you pick yourself, for example when you attach a file to a card or link to a document. It cannot look at anything else on your Mac.

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising

You do not sign in to use Tablinum. The app contains no analytics tools, no crash reporting service of its own, no advertising and no third-party tracking code.

The internet

Tablinum itself does not connect to the internet. When you click a web address in a card, the app hands that address to your web browser, which opens it the way it opens any other link.

The helper for AI assistants

Tablinum includes an optional helper that lets an AI app on your Mac, such as Claude or Codex, read and change your boards when you ask it to. Nothing happens unless you set this up yourself by following the guide in the app.

  • The helper runs on your Mac and only talks to the Tablinum app on the same Mac. It opens no network connection.
  • The AI app you connect is a separate product. It usually sends what you ask it, and the cards it reads in order to answer, to the company that makes it. That is governed by that company's privacy policy, not this one.
  • You can disconnect the AI app at any time, and Tablinum works fully without it.

Purchases

Tablinum is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the purchase and payment, and does not share your payment details with the developer. Apple may provide the developer with sales figures that do not identify you. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Syncing and backups

Tablinum has no sync service of its own. If you keep your data folder somewhere that is synced or backed up, for example iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Time Machine, then that service handles the files under its own terms.

Children

Tablinum is not aimed at children, and since it collects no data, it collects none from them.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. Your data is the folder on your Mac, and it is under your control. If you write to the address below, your email is used only to answer you.

Changes to this policy

If Tablinum ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
