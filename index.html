A kanban app for the Mac

Your boards, kept as plain files on your Mac.

Boards hold lists, and lists hold cards. Tablinum saves all of it as ordinary Markdown files in a folder you choose, so your work is always yours to read, back up and keep.

  • macOS 12 or later
  • Mac with Apple silicon
  • No account needed

Simple where it can be, clever where it helps.

Everything happens on your Mac. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sync unless you want to.

Files you own

Every card is a Markdown file with its attachments next to it. Open them in any editor, back them up, or keep the folder in iCloud Drive.

Lists that act on their own

Give a list a short script and it sorts its cards by deadline, passes them on, refuses the wrong ones or changes color when it fills up.

Dates you cannot miss

Start dates and deadlines change color as they come closer. You decide when, card by card, down to the minute.

Undo, copy and paste

Take back your last fifty changes, and copy cards or whole lists with the keys you already know. Deleted things wait in a trash.

Works with your AI assistant

Connect an assistant such as Claude or Codex and ask it to add, move or tidy up cards. The connection runs on your Mac only.

Private by design

Tablinum collects nothing and makes no connection to the internet. It can only reach the folder and the files you pick yourself.
